# Consent and Reuse Policy

This dataset is based on secondary data from Pérez-Chada et al.

## Consent
Original data collection was performed under the ethical guidelines of the primary study, including informed consent where applicable. As this is a recoded, anonymized version of publicly available data, no new consent is required for its use.

## Reuse
This dataset is released under the **CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication**. You are free to copy, modify, distribute, and perform the work, even for commercial purposes, all without asking permission.

See `CONSENT.md` for more details.
