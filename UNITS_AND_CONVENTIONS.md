# Units and Conventions

## Standard Units
- **Time**: All durations are expressed in **hours** (decimal format) unless otherwise specified.
- **Age**: Expressed in **years**.
- **Scores**: Academic performance scores follow the local scale of 1-10.

## Conventions
- **Date Format**: ISO 8601 (YYYY-MM-DD) where applicable.
- **Numeric Precision**: 2 decimal places for continuous variables.
- **Encoding**: UTF-8.
