# Project Governance

## Decision Making
Decisions regarding the dataset, including updates and changes to the schema, are made by the Principal Investigator (**Juan Moisés de la Serna Tuya**).

## Contributions
Community contributions are welcome via Pull Requests, subject to review and approval by the PI.

## Transparency
All changes are documented in the `CHANGELOG.md` and `RELEASE_NOTES.md`.
