# Sensitive Fields Registry

The following fields have been identified as potentially sensitive and have undergone de-identification or aggregation:

| Field Name | Sensitivity Level | Treatment |
|------------|-------------------|-----------|
| Age        | Low               | Categorized/Aggregated |
| Gender     | Low               | Anonymized |
| School ID  | Medium            | Masked/Removed |
| Location   | Medium            | Generalized to Region |

No PII (Personally Identifiable Information) is included in this dataset.
