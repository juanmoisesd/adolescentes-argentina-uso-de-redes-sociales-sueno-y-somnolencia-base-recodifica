# Bias and Limitations

## Data Bias
- **Selection Bias**: The original study focused on specific schools and regions in Argentina, which may not be representative of the entire national population.
- **Self-Reporting Bias**: Data on sleep and screen use were self-reported by adolescents, which can be subject to recall bias or social desirability bias.

## Limitations
- **Cross-Sectional**: The data represents a snapshot in time, preventing causal inferences.
- **Secondary Data**: As a recoded version, some nuances of the original raw data may have been simplified.

See `docs/limitations.md` for more details.
