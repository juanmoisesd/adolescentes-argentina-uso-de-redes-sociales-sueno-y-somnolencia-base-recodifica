# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### Is this data free to use?
Yes. Released under CC0 1.0 Universal — no restrictions.

### How do I cite this dataset?
See `CITATION.cff` or `CITATION.bib` in the root folder.

### How accurate is the data?
Compiled from authoritative secondary sources. See `QUALITY_REPORT.md` and `docs/validation.md`.

### Can I contribute or report errors?
Yes! Open an issue on GitHub or contact juanmoises.delaserna@unir.net.
