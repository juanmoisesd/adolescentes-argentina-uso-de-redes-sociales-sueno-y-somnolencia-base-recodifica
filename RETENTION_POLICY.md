# Data Retention Policy

## Retention Period
This dataset will be maintained and available for a minimum of **10 years** from the date of its initial release (2026).

## Archiving
After the retention period, the dataset may be moved to a long-term storage archive or deleted if it is no longer scientifically relevant.

## Updates
Updates to the dataset will be versioned and the previous versions will be archived for at least 5 years.
